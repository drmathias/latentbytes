I'm Adam, a technologist with a passion for quality, or as others might describe me, a stickler for well thought out specs and following established standards. Most of my career has been spent working on projects where quality and reliability are critical — electronic money, payments, and compliance systems, with a brief stint in blockchain development.

During this time early on in my career, I started a blog named Develop Momentum. Reading other developers' writing was a big part of my own growth, and part of what inspired me to start sharing my own thoughts there.

This blog is a spin-off from that — a more personal space to write about the topics that interest me now. After a break from writing, I've decided to start again, and this is somewhere to share thoughts more broadly. You'll find ramblings about computing, software and maybe the odd post about economics. I hope you find it useful.
